## Notes

### P0-2-NOTE
`exit-replay.engine.ts` is used in forensics/tests and settlement exit mapping. It is **not** imported by `src/server/simulation/backtest.service.ts`. Native position-monitor exits are classified via `mapPositionMonitorExit` at settlement. This does not fail P0.2 acceptance (deterministic non-`END_OF_REPLAY` exits are proven in unit tests).

### AI gate semantics (explicit)
- **Default:** `EXECUTION_AI_GATE_POLICY=VETO` → `AI_GATE_BLOCK` on `NO_TRADE`/`HOLD`/`REJECT`/`WAIT`.
- **Optional:** `EXECUTION_AI_GATE_POLICY=ADVISORY` + paper `learningLane` + `microTradeEligible` → `AI_ADVISORY_ONLY` with explicit reason `LEARNING_LANE_EXPLORATION_MICRO_TRADE` (not silent).
